Platform Guide & Policies

HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward guide to buying, listing, shipping standards, escrow protection, and receiving seller banking payouts on MillionTCG.

01 / MARKETPLACE
Transparent 10% Fee
Sellers keep 90% of every sale. There are no hidden insertion fees, monthly subscriptions, or surprise deduction charges.
02 / PROTECTION
Escrow Safe Hold
Buyer payments are securely held in escrow until the seller uploads valid tracking and the package is marked delivered by the carrier.
03 / PAYOUTS
Direct Seller Payouts
Upon verified delivery, net funds (90%) are directly disbursed to the seller via Direct Deposit (ACH), Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, or Wire.

STEP-BY-STEP The Marketplace Lifecycle

How transactions move from listing to final delivery and payout.

01

Listing Your Cards

Sellers publish singles, graded slabs, or sealed booster boxes using our listing portal. Sellers provide card title, category, set details, grade condition (Raw Mint, Near Mint, PSA 10, etc.), clear photos, and their asking price.

Our dynamic fee calculator automatically displays the exact 10% platform fee and your 90% net earnings before publishing.

Requirement: High-resolution photos showing front, back, and any visible corner/edge imperfections are mandatory for collector transparency.
02

Buyer Purchases & Funds Enter Escrow

When a buyer completes checkout, the payment is processed securely. The 10% platform fee is accounted for, and the remaining 90% net payout is placed into Escrow Protection (Status: Held in Escrow).

Both buyer and seller receive automated order confirmation receipts, and the order appears on the seller's dashboard awaiting dispatch.

Security Measure: Funds remain secured in escrow so sellers are guaranteed payment upon delivery, and buyers are protected against non-shipment.
03

Packaging & Shipping with Tracking

The seller prepares the package according to MillionTCG Top-Loader Protection Standards, ships the item via USPS, UPS, FedEx, or DHL, and enters the valid tracking number in their Seller Dashboard.

Once tracking is submitted, the order status transitions to In Transit, and real-time tracking is made accessible to the buyer.

Timeline: Sellers must dispatch packages and upload tracking within 2 business days of the order date.
04

Delivery Confirmation & Payout Disbursement

When the carrier confirms that the package has been delivered to the buyer's destination, the escrow status updates to Delivered & Released.

The administration verifies the confirmed delivery scan and directly transmits the 90% net funds to the seller's designated payout account (Venmo, Direct Deposit ACH, Zelle, or PayPal).

Confirmation: Sellers receive an email notification once their payout has been issued.

BANKING Payout Channels & Verification

Configure how you receive your 90% net earnings in your Account Dashboard.

Direct Deposit (ACH) Bank Transfer
Direct electronic bank transfer using your 9-digit Routing Number and Checking/Savings Account Number. Settles within 1–2 business days.
Venmo Instant Handle
Sent directly to your registered Venmo username (@handle). Ideal for fast personal balance payouts.
Zelle Direct Bank
Transferred directly to your bank account using your registered Zelle email address or mobile phone number.
PayPal Digital Wallet
Disbursed directly to your PayPal account email address. International and domestic compatible.
Wire Transfer High-Value Grails
Domestic wire transfer option reserved for high-value card and case sales exceeding $1,000 USD.
1099-K & Tax Compliance Encrypted
Encrypted Tax ID / SSN capture ensures regulatory compliance with IRS marketplace reporting thresholds.

How to set up your payout method:

Log into your account, navigate to Seller Banking & Payouts, choose your preferred payout channel (Venmo, Direct Deposit, Zelle, or PayPal), enter your legal name and account handle, and click Save Preferences. Your information is securely encrypted and synced across all your sessions.

SHIPPING Packaging Standards & Carrier Rules

Mandatory packaging protocol required of every seller on MillionTCG.

Item Category Required Protection Protocol Permitted Carriers Dispatch SLA
Raw Single Cards Soft penny sleeve + rigid ultra-clear top loader + team bag seal / painter's tape + bubble-cushioned mailer. USPS Ground Advantage / Priority, UPS, FedEx Within 48 hours
Graded Slabs (PSA/BGS/CGC) Slab protection sleeve + double layer bubble wrap + cardboard sandwich reinforcement + padded mailer or box. USPS Priority, UPS 2nd Day, FedEx Within 48 hours
Sealed Booster Boxes Original factory shrink-wrap verified + full bubble wrap surround + rigid corrugated carton box with packing peanuts/paper. USPS Priority, UPS Ground, FedEx Home Within 48 hours
High-Value ($500+) Standard packaging protocol + signature confirmation upon delivery + full carrier declared value insurance. USPS Express / UPS Next Day / FedEx Within 24 hours

Packaging Guidelines:

• Never use regular Scotch tape directly on top-loader openings (use painter's tape or resealable team bags).
• Plain white envelopes (PWE) are not permitted for any order over $10 to guarantee tracked delivery.
• Failure to follow packaging guidelines resulting in transit damage makes the seller liable for full refund.

ASSURANCE Disputes, Inspection & Returns

Clear terms protecting both collectors and sellers.

3-Day Inspection Window
Upon confirmed carrier delivery, buyers have 72 hours to inspect the card condition against the seller's listing photos and description.
Authenticity Guarantee
Every card listed on MillionTCG is guaranteed 100% authentic. Counterfeit, re-sealed, or altered products result in immediate refund and account termination.
Seller Chargeback Protection
Because buyer payments are held in escrow, sellers are protected against fraudulent chargebacks once delivery with valid tracking is established.
