SHIPPING POLICY

How we package, protect, and deliver your trading card collectibles.

1. Packaging Protection Standards

At MillionTCG, we treat every single card as a collector’s item. We know how frustrating damaged cards are, which is why we enforce strict packaging protocols:

Single Cards ($10+): Sleeved in penny sleeve + rigid ultra-clear top loader + team bag seal + bubble mailer.
Sealed Booster Boxes & Packs: Double bubble-wrapped in heavy-duty corrugated shipping boxes.

2. Order Processing Times

All orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday are packaged and shipped the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on weekends will ship the following business day.

3. Shipping Options & Delivery Rates

• Free Express Shipping: Orders over $75 qualify for free insured trackable shipping (2-4 business days delivery time).

• Standard Insured Shipping: Flat rate of $4.99 for orders under $75.

• Priority Overnight Shipping: Available at checkout for urgent tournament drops.

4. Worldwide Shipping

We ship internationally to collectors around the globe. International packages are processed via tracked international shipping with full insurance coverage.
